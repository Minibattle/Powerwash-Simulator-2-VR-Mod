A mod for Powerwash Simulator 2 that implements VR. Designed to feel eloquent and add physical interactions to the game to make it feel like a native VR game.

# Main Features
## Nozzle Belt
A belt appears on your hip with the nozzles grabbable and attachable physically, just like in the first games official VR port.


## Washer Backpack
A backpack is located over your offhand shoulder. Inside you can change your washer, nozzle, and extension by using the radial menu's that change based on what's being held.
