A mod for Powerwash Simulator 2 that implements VR. Designed to feel eloquent and add physical interactions to the game to make it feel like a native VR game.

# Main Features
## Nozzle Belt
A belt appears on your hip with the nozzles grabbable and attachable physically, just like in the first game's official VR port.

<img width="800" height="450" alt="2026-10-2916-25-38-ezgif com-video-to-gif-converter" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/776ce403-5182-45cc-8a97-eafb98fba837" />


## Washer Backpack
A backpack is located over your offhand shoulder. Inside you can change your washer, nozzle, and extension by using the radial menu's that change based on what's being held.

<img width="800" height="450" alt="backpack" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0260ac6d-bac5-4d19-87ae-5e1a358ff908" />


## Multiplayer Syncing
All interactions sync and appear amongst other players who have the mod. You can see them grab there backpack, clipboard, or if they're left handed. Players with the mod who aren't in VR can still see these things as well.

<img width="800" height="450" alt="multiplayer" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/40bdda1a-f88d-4367-b802-bfbe0aa2773b" />

## Physical Dirt Scanning
Hold your offhand up to your head and click trigger to scan for dirt.

<img width="800" height="450" alt="2026-10-2916-55-51-ezgif com-video-to-gif-converter" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0519b8eb-aafd-4fd3-bffe-ed16a319522d" />


## Finger Tracking and Steam Frame Support
There's full individual finger tracking and a custom control configuration to take advantage of the steam frame. There's also control preset's in the settings for other headsets.

<img width="800" height="450" alt="fingers" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9b92cc78-ee41-415e-ae3a-217641ae36da" />



# Other Features
- A LOT of settings
- VR settings don't interfere with flatscreen settings
- A clipboard is located on your washer hand's back where you can see uncompleted tasks
- Smooth camera option
- Limited support for players without the mod
- Left handed mode
- Control Presets
- Auto-Detect Steamvr setting for easy flat screen switching
- Character cosmetics refelct on your backpack and hands

# Known Issues
- Some grass textures appear smeary. This is because they would only render in one eye and this was the best solution I could find. Might look into this later.
- There's no way to edit furniture in VR. Might implement later if people actually care for it.

# Install
Grab the latest [Release](https://github.com/Minibattle/Powerwash-Simulator-2-VR-Mod/releases) and run the "install.cmd". That's it!
